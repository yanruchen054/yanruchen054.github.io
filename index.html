I am a Ph.D. candidate at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Tajana Rosing in the Systems Energy Efficiency Lab. My research focuses on computer architecture and hardware–software co-design, including heterogeneous memory, processing-in-memory, and efficient AI systems.

My recent work covers agentic AI inference, edge LLM acceleration, graph dynamic programming, and computational storage. I was an Emerging Memory AI Infrastructure Intern at SK hynix in summer 2026. I received my M.S. from Tsinghua University and B.E. from Jilin University.

I am seeking full-time positions in computer architecture, AI systems, and memory systems. Expected graduation: June 2027.
