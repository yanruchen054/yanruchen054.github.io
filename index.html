I'm a Ph.D. candidate at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Tajana Rosing in the Systems Energy Efficiency Lab. I study computer architecture and hardware–software co-design for efficient AI and data-intensive computing.

My work connects emerging memory technologies with the systems that use them—from agentic AI inference and edge LLMs to graph analytics and in-storage databases. In summer 2026, I worked on AI memory systems at SK hynix. Previously, I received my M.S. from Tsinghua University and B.E. from Jilin University.

Graduating June 2027. Seeking industry research and architecture roles in AI systems, accelerators, and memory. Let's connect
