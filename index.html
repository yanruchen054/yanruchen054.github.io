Yanru Chen

Ph.D. Candidate · Electrical and Computer Engineering
University of California San Diego

I am a Ph.D. candidate at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Tajana Rosing in the Systems Energy Efficiency Lab (SEELab). My research focuses on computer architecture and hardware–software co-design, particularly processing-in-memory, heterogeneous memory systems, and efficient AI and graph computing.

In summer 2026, I was an Emerging Memory AI Infrastructure Intern at SK hynix, where I worked on GLAIM and developed its trace-driven simulator for agentic AI inference. Previously, I received my M.S. from Tsinghua University and B.E. from Jilin University, with research in flexible electronics and medical image analysis.

I anticipate graduating in June 2027 and am seeking roles in computer architecture, performance modeling, and AI systems.

Emerging Memory AI Infrastructure Intern

Modeled hierarchical scheduling and compatible-request batching, with reproducible trace collection and replay across five agentic and retrieval benchmarks; this work led to the first-author GLAIM manuscript, submitted to ASPLOS 2027.
